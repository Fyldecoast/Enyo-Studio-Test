How to price with confidence

Why most founders underprice

They calculate cost per unit from the manufacturer and add a small margin, ignoring packaging, shipping, fees and marketing. By the time an order ships, profit evaporates.

Why 3x markup is often necessary

A 3x markup is not greed. It is survival. It accounts for returns, discounts, wholesale and the inevitable costs you didn’t plan for.

Why you must include marketing cost

Customer acquisition isn’t free. If you don’t budget for it per unit, you’ll either stop marketing or stop making money.

Why packaging matters

Branded packaging is a marketing channel. It costs more, but it drives repeat purchases, unboxing content and perceived value.
