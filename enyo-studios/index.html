FAQ’s

Questions, answered

How up-to-date is the manufacturer list?

We audit and update our directory monthly. Factories change MOQs, update contact details, or shift their specialties, so we continuously vet active suppliers, remove dead contacts, and add new verified manufacturers to keep your sourcing hassle-free.

How do the 1-on-1 coaching calls work?

1-on-1 coaching is exclusive to our Mentorship & Build Studio (£150.00 / month) members. Once subscribed, you get direct access to book private 1-on-1 strategy sessions, tech pack audits, and design reviews via your member portal. We cover everything from refining garment specs before factory submission to sample feedback, collection planning, and web coaching to help you plan and launch your own website.

How do I edit the tech packs after downloading?

All our tech packs are provided as fully editable Canva templates alongside ready-to-use PDFs. Simply click the Canva link included in your purchase to open the files in Canva (free to use), where you can change garment colors, add logos, adjust measurement tables, and modify design details.

  • Tier 1 (The Independent Founder): Includes 3 essential core tech pack templates.
  • Tier 2 (Studio Pro) & Tier 3 (Mentorship): Includes unlimited access to our entire expanded multi-style tech pack library.
Can I use these templates for any type of clothing?

Yes! Our tech pack templates are fully customizable vector blueprints designed to cover everything from everyday basics (t-shirts, hoodies, joggers) to complex, high-detail garments (multi-panel cut-and-sew pieces, outerwear, specialized activewear, and detailed hardware construction). You can adjust shapes, panels, and spec fields in Canva for any clothing style.

Can I cancel or upgrade my membership subscription at any time?

It depends on your plan, because only two of the three are subscriptions.

  • The Independent Founder (£29.99 one-off) is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You pay once, with no recurring fees, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. It includes instant digital download and lifetime download access to what is in the plan.
  • The Studio Pro (£59.99 / month) and Mentorship & Build Studio (£150.00 / month) are monthly. You can manage, upgrade, or cancel at any time directly through your account dashboard or Stan Store receipt link. If you cancel, you keep full access to your tier’s portal and tools until the end of your current billing cycle—no hidden cancellation fees or long-term commitments.

If you upgrade from The Studio Pro to Mentorship & Build Studio, your new features and booking access unlock instantly.
