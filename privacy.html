Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23rd 2026

Enya Studios ("Enya Studios," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access or use Enya Studios' website, platform, tools, and services.

By using Enya Studios, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

JOB APPLICANT INFORMATION

If you apply for a role, we collect the information described in section 13.

ACCOUNT INFORMATION

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Your name (the display name you choose)
  • Email address
  • Login information: we do not use passwords. We email you a one-time login code each time you log in
  • Account preferences
  • Your plan and its status (which membership you have, and when it starts or ends)
  • Posts and replies you write in the members' community

MEMBER DATABASE

Your account details above are stored in a database provided to us by Supabase, a hosted database and login service. Our database is hosted in the UK (London). We use it to log you in by email code, to match your plan to your email address, to run the members' community, and to hold the members-only resources (tech pack templates, manufacturer information, guides, the launch roadmap and class listings). Those resources are sent to you only after you log in and only if your plan includes them.

Your roadmap ticks, notes, to-dos, calendar entries and similar personal workspace items are saved in your own browser on your device, not in our database.

BILLING AND PAYMENT INFORMATION

When you purchase a plan, checkout is handled by Stan Store and payment processing by third-party providers such as Stripe. We receive your name and email address from the checkout and use your email address to switch on your plan. We may receive and store certain billing-related information such as Customer IDs, Subscription IDs, Checkout/session IDs, Payment status, and Billing plan or purchase history. Enya Studios does not store full payment card details directly unless explicitly stated.

UPLOADED CONTENT

You may upload content to Enya Studios, including reference images, inspiration photos, product visuals, design references, written prompts, notes, and creative descriptions. By uploading media to Enya Studios, you agree to our Media Upload Agreement, which sets out your responsibilities regarding ownership, consent for identifiable people, third-party rights, and prohibited content.

GENERATED CONTENT

Enya Studios may store or process content generated through the platform, including fashion sketches, garment imagery, e-commerce outputs, campaign visuals, ghost mannequin outputs, saved generations, and creative history.

SUPPORT AND BUG REPORT INFORMATION

If you contact support or submit a bug report, we may collect issue descriptions, screenshots, browser/device information, page URLs, usage context, and related account or billing details.

USAGE AND TECHNICAL INFORMATION

We may collect technical and usage-related information such as browser type, device type, IP address or approximate location, operating system, page visits, clicks and interactions, generation requests, platform usage history, and error logs and diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Provide and operate Enya Studios
  • Create and manage your account
  • Process subscriptions, billing, and credit purchases
  • Generate and store your creative outputs
  • Support saved history and workflow continuity
  • Troubleshoot bugs and technical issues
  • Improve product quality, reliability, and performance
  • Personalize the user experience
  • Communicate important service or account updates
  • Send optional marketing or product updates if you opt in
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How Uploaded and Generated Content Is Used

Enya Studios may process your uploaded and generated content in order to generate creative outputs, save your work to your account, restore your account history where applicable, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve system reliability and user workflows.

We do not claim ownership of your original uploaded or generated creative work as described in our Terms of Service.

4. Payments and Third-Party Payment Processors

Payments are taken at checkout on Stan Store and may be processed by third-party providers such as Stripe. When you make a purchase, certain payment and billing data may be handled by those providers according to their own privacy and security practices.

Enya Studios may store billing-related metadata needed to verify purchases, fulfill credits, manage subscriptions, and support account restoration or billing troubleshooting.

5. Marketing Communications

If you choose to opt in, we may send you Enya Studios updates, new tool announcements, platform improvements, product education, brand-building resources, and promotional communications.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting us.

We may still send non-marketing emails related to account activity, billing, support, service notices, and important platform changes.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

We may share information with trusted service providers or partners that help us operate Enya Studios, such as providers involved in hosting, infrastructure, authentication, analytics, payment processing, support tooling, storage, and generation systems or APIs. In particular: Supabase (database, login and file storage), our email delivery provider (which sends your login codes), and Stan Store with its payment providers (checkout).

Job application materials are not shared with generation systems or AI services.

We may also share information to comply with legal obligations, to protect rights, safety, or security, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or business transfer.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain account continuity, preserve saved creative history, troubleshoot issues, and fulfill legal, billing, security, and operational obligations.

Job applicant information is kept as described in section 13.

If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your login, display name, and community posts and replies from the database. A record of your plan (email address, plan, and dates) is held separately for billing and support purposes and is removed when you ask us to.

Some information may remain in backups, logs, or operational records for a period of time even after account changes or deletion requests.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and for using strong account security practices.

13. Job Applicants

When you apply for a role at Enya Studios, we collect your name, email, optional phone number, country and time zone (we may suggest these from your browser's time zone setting, and you can change them), LinkedIn profile, work links and social handles you provide, resume, portfolio links and files, answers to role questions (including your availability and how you heard about us), and your consent with the date and wording you agreed to.

We use limited technical information, including a protected identifier derived from your IP address, to prevent spam. This identifier is deleted after about two days.

We use this information only to review your application, contact you about this role and future roles that may fit, and keep private hiring notes. We do not use AI tools to screen or score applications, and we do not sell applicant information.

We keep applications, resumes and portfolios for 12 months after you apply, then delete them, unless you are hired. Copies may remain in secure backups or email records for a limited time before they are overwritten. Hired applicants' information may be retained as part of employment records.

You can ask us to delete your application, resume and portfolio at any time by emailing Enyaa.uk@gmail.com. We will delete them within 30 days.

Applicant files are stored privately with our hosting provider and are only visible to authorized Enya Studios administrators. When we email you about your application, our email provider processes your name and email address to deliver the message.
